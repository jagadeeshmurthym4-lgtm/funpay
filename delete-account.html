Summary: You can permanently delete your Fun Pay account and all associated data directly from within the app. Follow the simple steps below.

How to Delete Your Account

Deleting your account is a permanent action. Once confirmed, your account and all data associated with it cannot be recovered.

1 Open Fun Pay

Launch the Fun Pay app on your device.

2 Go to Account Management

Navigate to Profile → Account Management from the app's main menu.

3 Tap Delete Account

Scroll down to the Danger Zone section and tap Delete Account.

4 Confirm Deletion

Read the information carefully and tap Delete on the confirmation dialog to permanently delete your account.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Firebase Authentication account — your login credentials and email
  • Firestore user profile — your name, phone number, and profile information
  • Wallet and earnings data — your balance, transaction history, and reward records
  • Referral data — your referral code and referral history
  • Task and project submissions — your completed tasks and participation records

What is Not Deleted Immediately

The following may be retained temporarily for legal or operational reasons:

  • Fraud reports and login attempt logs — retained for security monitoring
  • Anonymized statistics — aggregated data that cannot be traced back to you
⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Make sure to withdraw any remaining wallet balance before deleting your account, as all pending and future earnings will be forfeited.
