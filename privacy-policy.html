Privacy Policy

Fun Pay

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Summary: Fun Pay collects limited data to provide rewards, tasks, referrals, and ads. We never sell your personal information. This policy explains what we collect and why.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign up, we collect:

  • Email address — used for account login, authentication (including Google Sign-In), and communications
  • Display name — optional, shown in your profile
  • Profile photo — optional, if you choose to upload one
  • Firebase User ID (UID) — a unique identifier assigned to your account

1.2 Device & Usage Information

  • Device model and OS version — for app functionality and crash reporting
  • App version — to ensure compatibility
  • Device language preference — to show the app in your preferred language
  • Network connectivity status — to manage offline/online behavior
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads (in compliance with Google Play policies)

1.3 Activity & Reward Data

  • Tasks completed — to track earnings and reward progress
  • Referral information — who referred you and your referral activity
  • Points, coins, and wallet balance — to manage your rewards
  • Withdrawal history — to process payout requests
  • Scratch card and spin game results — for gameplay functionality
  • Ad interaction data — to credit you for watching ads

1.4 Communications

  • Support tickets and messages — when you contact our support team
  • Notification preferences — to send you relevant alerts
  • Consent records — to document your preferences for data processing

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process rewards, points, and withdrawals
  • Assign and track task completion
  • Operate referral and bonus programs
  • Display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob
  • Send push notifications about new tasks, rewards, and promotions
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

Fun Pay uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing data handling:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Cloud Firestore (database), Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging (push notifications), and Crashlytics (crash reporting). Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: In-app advertising. AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication service. Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary: Image hosting and delivery for profile photos. Cloudinary Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) with servers primarily located in the United States. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firebase security rules to restrict data access
  • Authentication requirements for all API access
  • Secure storage of authentication tokens

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal or fraud prevention purposes.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access: You can view your profile data and transaction history in the app
  • Correction: You can update your profile information
  • Deletion: You can delete your account from within the app settings
  • Opt-out of notifications: You can disable push notifications in your device settings
  • Opt-out of ads: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
  • Data portability: Contact us to request a copy of your data

7. Children's Privacy

Fun Pay is not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app or via email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
